01 / Bahari House

A second life for a familiar building.

An adaptive reuse project in Mombasa, where the brief was to make a tired commercial building feel open, useful, and ready for another generation.

Atelier QS supported the team through cost planning, procurement, and final account, keeping the original character of the building in the conversation.

02 / Lakefront Commons

Public value, measured carefully.

A civic feasibility study for a new public gathering place in Kisumu. The work brought community aspirations into the same room as programme, budget, and long-term maintenance.

The result was a realistic phasing strategy that let the project start small and grow with confidence.
