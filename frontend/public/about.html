Hi, I’m Amina.

A little context

The work is technical.
The impact is human.

I started Atelier QS because the best project advice should feel like a conversation, not a performance. After more than a decade across commercial, residential, and civic work, I know that numbers are only useful when people can understand them and act on them.

I work with architects, developers, contractors, and community-minded clients who care about making places that last. My role is to make the path clearer, the risks visible, and the ambition possible.

“A budget is not a ceiling. It is a promise to use resources with intention.”
